OWNER’S GUIDE · NOT LINKED FROM THE PUBLIC SITE

Your new SitePilots site.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, package manager or framework required.

Open the website

1. Open or publish

Extract the ZIP and open index.html in your browser. To publish, upload the site files to the public directory of your static host. Keep the filenames and relative paths together. SETUP.html is this guide; you can omit it from the deployed site.

The website works locally, including its example selector, demos and email enquiry flow. Google Fonts needs internet access; system fonts are used if it is unavailable. The garden image is embedded in imagery.css, so there are no separate image files to lose.

2. Whop payments are connected

The payment button uses your live Website Build checkout: Open Whop checkout. Its URL is saved in config.js. Whop collects the payment on its own website; no secret keys or backend are needed.

What to do when someone pays

  1. Check the order in your Whop dashboard and confirm it is paid. Do not rely on a customer screenshot as proof.
  2. Use the email address on the order to contact the customer. Ask for their business name, services, service area, phone number, logo, photos and any existing domain details. Match the order to the brief you agreed.
  3. Confirm the delivery date and any outstanding content, then build the website.
  4. Email a preview for review, agree the final changes and arrange launch.

The website tells customers you will follow up by email. This is a manual workflow: no automatic onboarding email, order verification, customer account or payment-success redirect has been added. Check Whop for new orders regularly. A completed checkout is not reported back to this static site.

Before advertising, open checkout and verify the final price and tax settings. Make sure hello@sitepilots.co.uk is a working mailbox you monitor. Agree the brief, delivery date and separate hosting/domain costs before asking a customer to pay.

3. How enquiries work

The form validates the input and prepares an email. The visitor chooses “Open my email draft” and sends it from their email app. Nothing is submitted to a server and no enquiry is silently saved. The visitor can also copy the enquiry, with a selectable-text fallback if browser clipboard access is unavailable.

This avoids pretending a static form has submitted successfully. For automatic delivery, connect a form service or backend later and update the privacy information. The contact email comes from config.js for generated drafts; visible email links are in the HTML and must be updated too if you change address.

4. What you are offering

The site presents a £69 upfront build with pages and scope agreed before payment. There is no fixed one-page limit. Domain registration, hosting and ongoing updates are described as separate costs because their prices have not been supplied. Specialist features require a quote. No invented guarantees, testimonials or sales statistics are included.

Confirm these offer details, delivery process, ongoing prices and your sales/cancellation terms before advertising. The included privacy page describes this static build’s behaviour; update it to reflect your actual hosting, email handling, retention practices and any future analytics. It is not a complete tailored legal policy.

5. The website examples

example.html supports three variants selected by ?trade=landscaping, ?trade=electrical or ?trade=building. The fictional brands are Green & Ground, Volt Electrical and Hart & Co. Their contact sections return visitors to SitePilots rather than collecting leads for fictional businesses. The garden photo is AI-generated and illustrative. Do not represent these examples as completed client work.

The Boxhill Gardening & Tree Services portfolio link and screenshot have been preserved from your existing site. The new concept examples are separate from that existing portfolio project. The existing SitePilots social-preview image URL is retained. If you replace the domain’s files, retain sitepilotslogo.png at its current public URL or update/remove the og:image and twitter:image tags in index.html.

Whop Pixel

Your exact Whop Pixel snippet is installed once inside the head of index.html, example.html and privacy.html, scoped to biz_jXK5mqldnavWMX. It records page views. No extra purchase events are sent because Whop tracks its own checkout. The owner-only setup guide is not tracked.

After deploying, visit https://www.sitepilots.co.uk, then open Whop Websites and confirm your domain appears with a recent Last event. The ZIP itself cannot confirm receipt of live events.

Design research

The redesign applies scale, visual hierarchy, balance, contrast and proximity: strong type, a clear blue accent, varied section layouts and working examples rather than repeated decorative cards.

  • Nielsen Norman Group: 5 Principles of Visual Design in UX
  • Nielsen Norman Group: The Aesthetic-Usability Effect
  • Nielsen Norman Group: Proximity Principle in Visual Design
