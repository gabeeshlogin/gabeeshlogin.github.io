I already have Instagram. Do I need a website as well?

Instagram shows your work, but people searching Google for a salon near them find websites first. A site gives them your prices, your booking link and your reviews in one place.

Can clients book through the website?

Yes. We link to your existing booking system, such as Fresha, Treatwell or Booksy, or add a WhatsApp button if you take bookings by message.

Can I use photos from my Instagram?

Yes, as long as they are your own photos of your own work.

Do you build websites for aesthetics clinics?

Yes. We set out treatments, practitioners and consultation booking clearly.

What does the £99 cover?

The design and build of your website. Domain registration, hosting and future updates are separate, and we explain those costs before you pay.
