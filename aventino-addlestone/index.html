A small Italian with a big welcome

Aventino is a cosy neighbourhood restaurant and cafe near Addlestone station. Come in for a pizza after work, a bowl of pasta with friends, or a long Sunday dinner with the family.

Kids have their own menu, there’s plenty for vegetarians, and well-behaved dogs are welcome inside.

  • Kids’ menu Yes
  • Vegetarian options Yes
