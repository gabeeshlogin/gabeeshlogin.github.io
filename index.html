04 / FAIR QUESTIONS

Before
you ask.

Something else on your mind?
Just ask us.

What does the £99 cover?+

The design and build of your agreed business website. We confirm your pages, content and requirements before taking payment. Domain registration, hosting and ongoing updates are separate, so you can see the whole cost first.

Is there a limit on the number of pages?+

We focus on what your business needs rather than a rigid page allowance. We agree a sensible structure with you before starting. Specialist features such as an online shop or booking system are quoted separately.

Why is the price so low?+

SitePilots is a small, independent service with low overheads. We use modern development tools and keep the process focused. The £99 offer is for a straightforward business website with an agreed brief.

Do I need to supply anything?+

Your business name, service area, contact details, a list of services and any photos you want to show. Send your logo if you have one. Only share photos and other material you have permission to use.

When will it be ready?+

We’ll agree a delivery date when we know what you need and have your content. That way you get a realistic date for your project, rather than a promise made before we’ve seen the work.

How do payment and hosting work?+

Once we agree your brief and any separate domain or hosting costs, use the Whop button below to pay for your £99 build. We’ll check your order and contact you using your checkout email address to collect your business details, logo and photos. We then build your site and send you a preview to review. Already paid?
