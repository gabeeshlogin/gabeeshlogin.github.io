FAQs

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email us and we’ll get back to you.

What does the £99 cover?

The design and build of your agreed business website. We confirm your pages, content and requirements before taking payment. Domain registration, hosting and ongoing updates are separate, so you can see the whole cost first.

Is there a limit on the number of pages?

We focus on what your business needs rather than a rigid page allowance, and agree a sensible structure with you before starting. Specialist features such as an online shop or booking system are quoted separately.

Why is it so affordable?

SitePilots is a small, independent service with low overheads. We use modern tools and keep the process focused. The £99 price is for a straightforward business website with an agreed brief.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, service area, contact details, a list of services and any photos you want to show. Send your logo if you have one. Only share photos and material you have permission to use.

How long will it take?

We’ll agree a delivery date once we know what you need and have your content. That way you get a realistic date for your project, not a promise made before we’ve seen the work.

How do payment and hosting work?

Once we’ve agreed your brief and any separate domain or hosting costs, you pay for your £99 build securely through Whop. We then email you at your checkout address to collect your details, logo and photos, build your site and send you a preview. Already paid? Email us with your Whop order reference if you need help.
