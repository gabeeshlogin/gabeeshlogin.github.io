A restobar, as the locals call it

Nostalgia serves recipes from the Mediterranean alongside British favourites. Yiannis looks after the front, there is a drinks board and a specials board, and the drinks, laughter and good company matter as much as the food.

Come for a coffee and a pain au chocolat, stay for lunch from the specials, or come back on Friday night for meze and a glass of wine.
