From a Barbados fish truck to Reading town centre

Dolphin grew up in a village in Barbados, fascinated by the fish truck that came through calling “fish, fish, five for a dollar”.

At ten he and his friends bought some fish, took sweet potatoes and breadfruit from the garden and cooked over an open fire. He learned the traditional Bajan recipes watching his grandmother, and was soon cooking street food at local football and volleyball matches.

He brought his cooking to the UK in the 1990s, first in Wales and then Reading, where the West Indian community kept asking him for home-cooked meals. That became Dolphin’s Caribbean Catering. After around twenty years of catering, St Mary’s Butts is his first permanent restaurant.
