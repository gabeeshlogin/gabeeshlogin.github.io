BEFORE YOU VISIT.

Yes, there is a children’s menu served 3pm–5pm, available to eat in or take away.

Can I order a takeaway?

Takeaway is available. Call 07988 336974 to check availability and order.

Please call the restaurant to discuss your party size and preferred date.
