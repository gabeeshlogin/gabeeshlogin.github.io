Waterden Dental Practice - website concept (interactive demo)
=============================================================

Open index.html in any modern browser. No build step or server needed.

Files
- index.html      Page markup
- styles.css      All styles (brand colours sampled from the Waterden logo and team photography)
- script.js       Opening-hours badge, treatment filter and detail panels, team profiles,
                  reviews scroller, fee tabs, FAQ, question form and the demo booking flow
- logo-light.png  Logo for light backgrounds (navy "Dental Practice" line)
- logo-dark.png   Original logo for dark backgrounds
- *.jpg           Team headshots (13)

Brand colours
- Teal   #11A48E  (logo)        Teal for buttons/links #0A7A6A (passes contrast with white)
- Navy   #003366  (headings)
- Slate  #395465  (portrait backdrop, team section)

Demo behaviour
- The booking flow and question form are simulated. Nothing is sent or stored;
  entered details are cleared when the flow completes or closes.
- Appointment times shown are illustrative, not live availability.
- The page is marked noindex.

Before going live
- Replace the team photos with the original high-resolution files (these are cropped
  from screenshots at about 222x288px) and use a full-size logo file.
- Confirm permission to reuse the logo and photos.
- Confirm fees, team roles and opening hours with the practice.
- Connect "Book an appointment" to the practice's booking system
  (currently waterden-dental.portal.dental) and the question form to their email.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts (Outfit).
