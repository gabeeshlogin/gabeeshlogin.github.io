A few things you might like to know.

What is Luxe Escape Co?

A luxury travel inspiration and discovery brand showcasing exceptional destinations, hotels, resorts and travel experiences.

Can I book a holiday through you?

At present the site offers travel inspiration, recommendations and links to selected travel partners. We don't currently take direct holiday bookings.

Do you work with hotels and travel brands?

Absolutely. We welcome collaborations with hotels, resorts, tourism boards, tour operators and experience providers.

Do you accept hosted stays and experiences?

Yes. We're interested in experiencing and showcasing exceptional properties and activities through authentic content and promotional partnerships.

Are recommendations sponsored?

Some content may be sponsored, hosted or supported by affiliate partnerships. Where it is, we say so clearly, so you can make informed decisions.

Can you help me plan a holiday?

We'd love to hear from anyone looking for destination inspiration, hotel recommendations and ideas for their next escape. Any additional planning services will be explained clearly before you commit to anything.
