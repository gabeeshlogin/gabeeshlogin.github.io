The building came first.

Number 17A Castilian Street opened on 10 April 1921 as the Taylor Memorial Hall. Councillor David Paton Taylor paid for it in memory of his son, Lieutenant Ralph Paton Taylor, who was killed on the Somme in 1916.

The Dundee-born architect Alexander Ellis Anderson gave it turrets, a crow-stepped gable and a two-storey hall with balconies and stained glass, in the Scots Baronial style you rarely see this far south. It is Grade II listed.

Today the old two-storey main hall is our dining room, with a lot more pasta than it was built for.
