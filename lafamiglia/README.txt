La Famiglia (Northampton) - SitePilots concept site
====================================================

Live at (after push): https://sitepilots.co.uk/lafamiglia/

WHY THEM
- Italian restaurant, 17A Castilian Street, Northampton NN1 1JS
- Google: 4.8 stars from 357 reviews. Food hygiene rating 4 (21 March 2025).
- WhatsApp Business confirmed: 07916 249066 is listed on chatwith.io as their
  WhatsApp, and it's the same number on their Google listing and Facebook.

WHAT'S WRONG WITH THEIR CURRENT SITE (lafamiglia-restaurant.co.uk)
- Homepage hero says "About US" / "WARNING !!!" before saying what they are
- Menu page is two pictures only, no text (no prices in Google, unreadable on phones)
- Menu category links (Appetizer / Main Dish / Dessert) go nowhere ("#")
- Opening hours list Fri-Sun only; Google says Thu-Sun
- Three different phone numbers across header/footer
- "Christmas 2025" still in the main menu in late 2026; footer says (c) 2025
- Typos: "RESERVE OW", "Trufle", "Gharcoal-grilled", "Carvey"
- Counters show "0 +" and "0 %"; an image alt text is "rgfhggrefddgff"
- Book Now page is a bare link with no form or button text

WHERE EACH FACT ON THE NEW SITE CAME FROM
- Menu dishes + prices: their Spring 2026 menu image on their own site
- Steaks + "aged minimum 35 days, grilled over charcoal, finished at your table": same menu
- Hours: Google listing (Thu-Sat 5-11pm, Sun 12-8pm, Mon-Wed closed)
- "Live music ... after 20:00": their homepage
- Events, "no extra hire fees": their Events page
- Building history: Northamptonshire Historic Environment Record (MNN170673):
  opened 10 April 1921, architect Alexander Ellis Anderson, funded by
  Cllr David Paton Taylor in memory of Lt Ralph Paton Taylor (Somme, 1916),
  Scots Baronial, Grade II listed
- Reviews: Google (Richard Bignell, Taffy Chamwarura, Veaceslav Calmic),
  their own Events page (Lilia Borta, from Facebook). Quoted word for word,
  trimmed with ... where shortened.
- Booking link: their existing Tableo page https://app.tableo.com/r/f0e22GC
- Photos: hotlinked from their own website (their photos). Nothing stock.

CONFIRM WITH THEM BEFORE GOING LIVE
- Opening hours (site says Fri/Sat 4pm, Google says Thu-Sat 5pm)
- Which phone number they want shown (07916 249066 vs 01604 385060)
- Prices are from the Spring 2026 menu; they may have an autumn/winter menu now
- Permission to use the reviews and their photos (copy photos into the repo
  instead of hotlinking once they say yes)
- Whether there's live music every night they're open
