Privacy & cookies.

Here’s what happens when you browse this website or prepare an enquiry.

Your enquiry

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your own email app. Typing in the form does not send your details to SitePilots. You choose whether to send the email. If you do, the name, contact information and business details you include are shared with SitePilots so we can discuss your enquiry.

The website does not save your enquiry to a database or browser storage. Your browser may offer its own autofill features. Avoid including passwords, payment card information or other sensitive details in your message.

Cookies and analytics

This website loads the Whop Pixel from t.whop.tw on public pages to measure page views and help attribute visits and purchases to advertising. Your browser connects to Whop when the pixel loads. Whop may process technical and browsing information and use tracking technologies for attribution. The enquiry form does not send its fields to the pixel. See Whop’s privacy policy for information about its processing. Your hosting provider may also process technical information, such as an IP address, to serve the website.

Fonts and external websites

The main site and examples request fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to load them. Website examples use an embedded illustrative image. Links to Whop and other external sites open services with their own privacy information and policies.

Payments

If you follow a Whop payment link, you enter your payment details on Whop. This static website does not collect or store card details or confirm payment status.

Questions about your information

Contact hello@sitepilots.co.uk to ask about information you have shared with SitePilots.
